Everything, in one tab

Everything Word and Excel do — none of the bloat

Quillgrid isn't a stripped-down demo. It's a genuinely capable editor and spreadsheet, built to cover the features people actually use every day.

Formulas that just work

SUM, VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, IFS, COUNTIFS and 60+ more — the functions people actually reach for.

Charts in one click

Select a range and insert a bar, line or pie chart — no plugin, no export round-trip.

Real formatting

Bold, colors, borders, merged cells, number formats and conditional formatting — the whole toolbox.

Rich documents

Headings, lists, tables, images and links, in a page that prints and exports cleanly.

Multiple sheets

Tabs, freeze panes, sort and fill-down — the workbook mechanics you already know.

Import & export

Bring in a CSV, or export your sheet as CSV/HTML, or print straight to PDF.

Keyboard-first

Ctrl+B, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+D fill-down — every shortcut your hands already know.

Private by design

Everything autosaves to your own browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked.
